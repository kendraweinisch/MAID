# Business name shortlist

Domains checked through public RDAP lookups on 2026-11-17. "Free" means no
registration record at that moment; availability can change, so register a
pick soon. Run a USPTO trademark search before committing to any name.

| Rank | Name | .org | .com | Why | Watch out for |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | **End of Life Atlas** | free | free | Plain words people search; "atlas" says place by place; works beyond MAID (hospice, advance directives) | Long-ish; fine as `endoflifeatlas.org` |
| 2 | **MAID Atlas** | free | free | Exact clinical term people search; short | Readers who don't know the acronym may read "maid" |
| 3 | **Atlas of Choice** | free | free | Warmer, more brandable | Less obvious in search; needs a subtitle |
| 4 | **MAID Navigator** | free | free | Short, clear | Sounds like a service, not a neutral guide |
| 5 | Clear Passage | free | taken | Calm and evocative | Euphemistic; someone holds the .com |

## Chosen name

**End of Life Atlas**, a voluntary, information-only site. (An earlier idea of a
separate paid service under another name has been dropped.)

## Names to avoid

Anything using "Dignity," "Compassion," "Choices" (as in Compassion & Choices),
"Exit," "Final Exit," or "Lantern" (an existing end-of-life app): too close to
existing organizations, or tied to methods outside the law.

## Also checked (taken in one or both TLDs)

maidmap (.com taken), choiceatlas (.com taken), choicemap (both taken),
maidguide, endoflifeanswers, waypointguide, thresholdguide, openchoiceguide,
knownpath, mychoiceguide (.com taken for each). finalchoicesguide is free in
both but reads as euphemistic.
