# End-of-life planning competitors (the After Party space)

Checked 2026-11-17. Funding figures come from news and aggregator reports;
pricing marked "approx." is from general knowledge. Verify on each company's
site before quoting.

| Company | What it does | How it makes money |
|---|---|---|
| [After Party](https://planmyafterparty.com/) | Pre-launch. Funeral and legacy wishes, letters, children's grief workbooks, free printables | Nothing live. Plans "trusted services" and "curated finds" (urns) |
| Cake (joincake.com) | Free end-of-life planning profile and a large library of articles on death and grief | Search traffic plus affiliate and partner deals (approx.) |
| [Everplans](https://www.everplans.com) | Digital vault for wishes, documents and accounts; state guides | Free basic plan; paid premium; sold through financial advisors and insurers |

| GoodTrust | Wills, digital-account legacy, document vault | Paid tiers and B2B (approx.) |

| [Paige](https://www.heyfuturenexus.com/posts/funded-paige-raises-2-5m-to-bring-legacy-planning-into-the-fintech-stack) | Wills, vault, passwords, messages for loved ones | Fintech partners; raised $2.5M (Jun 2026) |

## Patterns

1. **Consumers rarely pay.** The money comes from insurers, employers,
   financial advisors and funeral sales. Families get the product free.
2. **Free content brings the visitors.** Cake and Ever Loved grew on search
   traffic from articles and memorial pages.
3. **The money is in the funeral.** Meadow makes the biggest money by selling
   cremations and funerals online, not planning tools.
4. **Vaults are common.** Wills, documents and passwords are already done well.
   Don't compete there.

## Gaps After Party and End of Life Atlas could fill

- Planning with personality: celebrations of life rather than estate admin.
- Children's grief materials (After Party's printables are unusual).
- Covering medical aid in dying as a real option. No planning app does this.
